Fuska

Fuska is a very popular street food in Bangladesh. It is made with small, crispy balls filled with mashed potatoes, onions and spices. These balls are served with sour and spicy water called "tok pani".











Kala Buna

Kala bhuna is a meat curry made of beef or mutton, originated in Chittagong, Bangladesh. Different types of spices are needed to prepare this traditional dish of Chittagong.
