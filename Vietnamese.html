Pho

Phở or pho is a Vietnamese soup dish consisting of broth, rice noodles, herbs, and meat – usually beef, and sometimes chicken. Phở is a popular food in Vietnam where it is served in households, street-stalls, and restaurants nationwide.











Canh Chua

Canh chua is a Vietnamese sour soup indigenous to the Mekong Delta region of Southern Vietnam. It is typically made with fish from the Mekong River Delta, pineapple, tomatoes, and bean sprouts, in a tamarind-flavored broth.
